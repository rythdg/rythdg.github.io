---
title: "Mechanics of E. coli cell width homeostasis and bulge dynamics"
order: 4
description: "E. coli are rod-shaped organisms, and their lengths increase constantly until they divide. However, their widths saturate with time. The question was – how, and why. I fit a biophysical shell-mechanics model to cell shape dynamics under drug perturbations, to characterize the mechanical stiffnesses of the bacterial cell wall, including limits at which the cells would burst. (CyCels Lab, IISER Pune)"
