description: "
Water-logging and flooding affects up to 30% of crop yield in India. Most crops begin to wilt under water-logged conditions within a day, and die after about 3 days. This happens because of hypoxia-driven over-production of ethylene in the roots. We genetically modified a naturally occurring soil bacterium Azospirillum *brasilense*, to be able to break-down ethylene precursors in the roots of plants in response to hypoxia, alleviating the toxic levels of ethylene, and extending crop-life.

This project was part of the International Genetically Engineered Machines (iGEM) competition.
Our team won the Gold Medal at the main even in Paris in 2022!"
