description: "Synaptic signalling involves thousands of molecules, but the largest network models cover only hundreds. The goal is to extend the lab's model using proteomics and literature data, then fit young, aged and Alzheimer's states to find common upstream dysregulations. To scale up model-building, I built Atelier, an AI-integrated pipeline that converts papers directly into protocols executable on the model."
