title: "Fuel homeostasis during exercise in ageing men"
order: 5
description: "As people age, their ability to maintain metabolic homeostasis reduces. The aim was to characterize how and what changes in the metabolism of humans as they age. I fit a multi-scale dynamical model of human metabolism to data from young men and older men, and compared what changes in their metabolism drive the experimental differences. (Layton Lab, University of Waterloo)"
